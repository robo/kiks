The night shift

Kiks sleeps all day and works all night. This is roughly what happens while everyone else is in bed.

  1. 7:30 pmWakes up. Enormous stretch. Sits still for a while thinking about it.
  2. 8:00 pmFirst lap on the wheel. Then two hundred more.
  3. 9:15 pmSnack raid — seeds go in the cheeks, cheeks go back to bed.
  4. 11:00 pmRearranges the entire house. The bedding was in the wrong corner.
  5. 2:00 amSerious digging. No one knows what he is looking for.
  6. 4:30 amTwelfth snack of the night.
  7. 6:00 amBack to bed. Do not disturb.

The snack menu

Tiny hamster, tiny portions — a piece the size of your fingernail is a whole meal for Kiks.

Yes please

  • Sunflower seeds — the absolute favourite
  • A sliver of cucumber
  • Plain oats
  • A little broccoli or carrot
  • One mealworm, as a treat

Never ever

  • Chocolate and sweets
  • Oranges, lemons, anything citrus
  • Onion and garlic
  • Almonds
  • Anything salty or from a packet

These can make him very ill. If you are not sure about a food, ask a grown-up before giving it to him.

House rules

Kiks is smaller than a phone and braver than he looks. This is how to be his favourite person.

  • Scoop him up with two flat hands, low over his bed.
  • Never grab a sleeping hamster — say hello first and let him wake up.
  • Fresh water every single day, no days off.
  • Quiet voices near the cage. His ears are enormous.
  • Wash your hands before, so you do not smell like snacks.
  • Dwarf hamsters live about two years. Make them good ones.
